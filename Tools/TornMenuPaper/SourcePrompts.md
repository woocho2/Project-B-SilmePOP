# Torn notebook menu paper

Mode: built-in image_gen edit with transparent alpha. Basic was edited from the existing menu PNG; the other seven themes were edited from Basic's torn-paper illustration. Each has ragged paper edges, open torn binding holes, faint ruling and a small themed doodle in the margins. System.Drawing is used only to fit the generated RGBA sprite into each existing active menu slice, preserving PNG dimensions and every original .meta file.

## Basic

Production UI MENU PAPER sprite for the existing hand-drawn notebook slime game. Transform the referenced rectangular framed menu into ONE actual sheet TORN OUT OF A NOTEBOOK. Portrait approximately 3:4 page, straight-on, upright, no perspective or rotation. Genuinely transparent alpha everywhere outside the paper, INCLUDING open torn binder holes. Fresh warm ivory paper with very faint pale blue horizontal notebook ruling and one extremely faint pale coral margin line near the left. Distinct irregular fibrous torn TOP and BOTTOM edges, and a torn-out LEFT spiral-binding edge with 6 to 8 clearly visible ripped punched holes open to the outside. Tiny asymmetrical paper nicks and very light soft pencil edge shading, subtle paper grain. NO rectangular outline, NO thick charcoal border, NO decorative picture frame, NO wooden board, NO clipboard, NO clip, NO tape, NO ring hardware, NO drop shadow. Paper face must remain VERY LIGHT and its central 80% entirely blank to receive real menu title/buttons later. No text, no lettering, no numbers, no buttons or slime character. Keep theme doodles SMALL and tucked into the FAR UPPER-RIGHT and LOWER-LEFT margins, never in the central button area. Full paper and every ragged edge safely inside canvas with small transparent margin. Match existing simple charcoal and colored-pencil doodles, not photorealism. BASIC theme doodles: one tiny fresh two-leaf GREEN SPROUT at upper-right, one tiny two-leaf sprout with a dew dot near bottom-left, restrained mint/leaf-green pencil. All green stays confined to those tiny doodles. Main paper is warm ivory with no green wash.
