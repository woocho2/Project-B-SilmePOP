# Crayon slider concept

Mode: built-in image_gen. Preview-only concept on warm-white paper; not a transparent production sprite and not connected to any scene or script. Saved as Tools/CrayonSlider/Concept.png.

## Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a clean concept drawing of ONE horizontal slider/progress bar for a hand-drawn crayon sketchbook game. The attached image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY for its colored-pencil outlines and wax-crayon grain; do not reproduce any of its characters, stationery or scenery. Primary request: a long simple RECTANGULAR BOX with square corners, its inside colored by ONE broad horizontal wax-crayon stroke. Thin dark graphite pencil rectangle outline, gently handmade imperfect lines but straight overall, no rounded pill shape, no thick frame, no bevel. Make the bar about 9 times as wide as tall, centered large on a plain warm-white lightly textured paper backdrop, landscape composition. Inside the box, one continuous MINT GREEN crayon stripe fills approximately 70 percent from LEFT to RIGHT, rightmost 30 percent remains uncolored paper. The stripe is nearly as tall as the interior, with a tiny breathing gap to the outline. Emphasize a tactile wax stroke made by dragging the broad side of a crayon horizontally: fine white paper-grain gaps, uneven pigment coverage, subtle parallel streaks running left-to-right, dense cheerful mint pigment with tiny varied pressure marks. Right edge of the colored stripe is softly ragged like an actual stroke ending, not a clean vector cut. Keep color inside the rectangle. The frame and fill should look like a child neatly drew a box on a sketchbook and colored a single line through it; readable restrained game UI, no elaborate decoration. Gentle consistent matte colors, minimal shading. No slider knob, no cursor, no icons, no text, no labels, no percentages, no numbers, no scale ticks, no full UI screen, no extra bars, no cast shadows, no shiny plastic or 3D rendering. One concept only, shown clearly at large scale.
